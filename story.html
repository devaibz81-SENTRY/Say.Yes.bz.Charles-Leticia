The Beginning

A Christmas Trip to Cayo

♥ ♡ ♥ ♡

Almost a decade ago, Charles decided he wanted a Cayo gyal and made his way to Cayo for the Christmas break. Little did he know, that trip would lead him straight to the love of his life.

While hanging out with friends, Charles was introduced to Leticia. And as luck would have it, she was from Cayo, just like he had hoped — but lived in Belize City. Charles only had to lay eyes on her once, and from that moment on, he never let her out of his sight.

"The funny part? They had been living in the same city, practically in each other's orbit, all along… yet somehow, they had never met. Clearly, the timing just wasn't right — until it was."
